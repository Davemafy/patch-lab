# Patch — All Gas build

## Product
Tell Patch what broke → find people who handle it → ask for real price/time → receive real replies → choose someone.

## Sponsor responsibilities
- Convex: real source of truth and live updates
- Firecrawl: source-backed public service evidence
- AgentMail: ordinary outbound/inbound email
- OpenAI: conservative interpretation of the repair and reply facts

## Truth boundary
A public website may show that someone handles a repair. Only a real reply may establish current availability, price, timing, or acceptance.

## Demo
Broken bedroom doorknob → real outreach → real reply → Patch updates live → user chooses.

Five-chat implementation in progress.
